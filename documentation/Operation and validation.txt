Operating notes — firmware V5.2.2

The laser source rotates around a stationary detector. Maintain alignment, keep the analogue input within 0-5 V, enclose the scan plane/use a beam stop and keep clear of the moving arm. The separate optical experiment's conditioned readout is not the Arduino input circuit.

The developer reports successful operation of the apparatus, controller and acquisition. Individual fault-test records and a reproducible binary build are not supplied.

Known historical firmware limitations identified by code inspection: STOP is not honoured during homing; physical Start has no homed-position guard; HOME and LASER OFF can disrupt active state handling. These controls are not a hardware emergency stop. Isolate power if motion cannot be stopped through the controls. Validate limits, alignment and command behaviour before a new acquisition.
